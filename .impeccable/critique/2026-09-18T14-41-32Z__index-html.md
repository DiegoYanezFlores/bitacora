## Overall Impression

This is a well-executed, disciplined personal tool that mostly delivers on its own stated brand law — the single-accent rule and the "explain everything" principle are not aspirational copy, they're verifiably what's live in production, in both themes. The biggest gap isn't taste, it's that a couple of shipped defaults quietly work against the product's own core loop: the FAB — the app's single most-used control — visually collides with content on three different screens, and the one screen built for the Monday reflection ritual defaults to the wrong week for that ritual.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] The floating action button collides with real content on three screens.** Confirmed independently by both assessments: on Today's empty state, the FAB visually covers the word "actividad" in the "Registrar actividad" button (renders as "Registrar  ctividad"); on Progreso it overlaps the "MAPA DE ACTIVIDAD" heading before scrolling; on Ajustes it sits over the "Vibración al completar" toggle row. **Why it matters:** the FAB is the app's single primary action, used many times a day per the product's own operating model — it shouldn't obscure the exact content a user needs to read at that moment. **Fix:** reserve bottom clearance for the FAB's actual footprint on every view (not just via the tab bar's padding), and consider dropping the redundant "Registrar actividad" empty-state button entirely since the FAB already offers the identical action one tap away. **Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout`

**[P1] Progreso opens to the current week, undermining the Monday reflection ritual the product is designed around.** PRODUCT.md defines the weekly session as "resumen de la semana anterior," but `progress.js` always initializes `state.range='week', state.offset=0` — the *current* week. On the actual Monday this ritual happens, that week is nearly empty, and the real user must manually tap back to see the week the ritual is about. **Why it matters:** this is a direct default-state contradiction of the one ritual the product's core loop was built around, for the exact real user described in PRODUCT.md. **Fix:** detect Monday (or a near-empty current period) and default `offset` to -1, or give the weekly-reflection entry point its own route pinned to last week. **Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify` (state/flow fix) or `/impeccable harden`

**[P2] "Meta semanal" in Ajustes is a flat 7-option picker for a setting its own copy calls low-stakes.** Exceeds the ≤4-visible-choices working-memory guideline; Registro's kind filter (5 pills + a separate project select as a second simultaneous axis) has the same shape of problem. **Why it matters:** adds friction to decisions the product explicitly wants to feel light ("no hay castigo si no llegas"). **Fix:** collapse to fewer buckets (e.g. 2/4/6/Personalizado) or a stepper for the weekly goal; consider merging Registro's two filter axes into one control. **Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout`

**[P2] Progreso → Récords shows a bare "racha actual: 0" at the same visual weight as "mejor racha."** This is the one place in the app where a zero gets full typographic parity with a personal best — Hoy, by contrast, deliberately hides the streak stat entirely when it's zero. **Why it matters:** it's a specific, concrete contradiction of the brand law that a shown number is never punitive, in an app whose entire premise is "no patrones oscuros, sin culpa." **Fix:** mirror the hiding/de-emphasis logic already used on Hoy. **Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify`

**[P2] The documented design-system scale covers a fraction of what's actually shipped.** DESIGN.md's 5-step typography ramp and 4-step radius scale are real and followed for primary surfaces, but the detector found 30 font-size and 9 radius declarations in `app/styles.css` outside those scales (small labels at 10-13px, secondary radii at 2-8px) — mostly legitimate, purposeful choices (dots, kbd, focus rings) rather than slop, but undocumented. **Why it matters:** without documented secondary steps, future edits (by you or an AI agent) have no scale to check against for anything below "card-level," which is exactly where drift compounds. **Fix:** either consolidate the CSS onto the documented scale where there's no real reason for a one-off value, or extend DESIGN.md with a documented secondary tier (e.g. `label-sm: 13px`, `radius.dot: 3px`) so the system describes what's actually intentional. **Suggested command:** `/impeccable document` (refresh) or `/impeccable typeset`

**Alex (Impatient Power User):** Enter-to-save in capture can commit before the 120ms-debounced auto-detected kind/project pill has settled, silently mis-filing an entry with no second look. The FAB/CTA overlap above is exactly the kind of visual noise that annoys someone moving fast.

**Casey (Distracted Mobile User):** Tabbar items 2 and 3 are pushed outward (28px margin) to clear room for the centered FAB — a rushed one-handed tap near bottom-center on a moving bus risks the wrong control. If Casey is interrupted mid-sentence while typing a capture, the live re-parse can flip the detected kind/project between the interruption and the return, and Casey — distracted — is the least likely user to notice before saving.

**Sam (Accessibility-Dependent User):** Positive: `:focus-visible` renders a clean, high-contrast ring in dark mode, verified live on the desktop sidebar. Risk: the activity heatmap and week dots encode intensity purely through accent-color saturation steps with no redundant channel (pattern or label), and only a hover `title` as a text alternative — a low-vision user browsing quickly on touch has no reliable way to distinguish "some activity" from "a lot."

**The Quito data engineer (project-specific — the actual real user):** Their real "Plan 12 meses" project (seen live) has no metric and 0% progress; turning the Next Action nudge ("Define el siguiente paso") into a measurable target requires navigating into project-edit and expanding the collapsed "Medir con un número" section — several taps removed from the moment of intent, which costs more for someone with genuinely few weekly hours. The Monday-defaults-to-current-week issue above directly undermines the one ritual this entire product exists to support for this exact user.

## Minor Observations

- Achievements only ever show unlocked + nearest 3 locked, with no way to browse all criteria if curious.
- The Google sign-in button correctly uses the one documented exception to the single-accent rule (unrecolored multicolor logo) — consistent with DESIGN.md's own Do/Don't list.
- Desktop (1280px, verified live) keeps content capped at 640px, leaving roughly half the screen empty — an explicit, documented "never multi-column" choice, worth questioning specifically for the one longer desktop-friendly session (Monday reflection) where more visible history might help.
- The 5-second undo toast is the only recovery path for deleted tasks/milestones/activities, with nothing after it expires — low risk for most flows, but worth a lightweight "recently deleted" list in Ajustes given how often this app is used in short, interruptible bursts.
- Production correctly ships a locked-down CSP (`script-src 'self'`, no inline/external scripts) — a real security positive, though it's why no automated visual overlay could be produced for this run.
